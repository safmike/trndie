The New Melbourne Bakeries and Cafes Worth Lining Up for This Autumn

Concrete Playground · 20 May 2026

Autumn 2026 has been generous to Melbourne's cafe scene. Three openings have shifted the inner-north's rhythm, and one Abbotsford holdover has somehow gotten more popular, not less.

Marrowbone (Fitzroy)

The seafood-residency-of-the-month is genuinely as good as the noise around it. Marrowbone is the kind of menu that rewards trust — order what the floor team is excited about, not what you came in planning to eat.

Cibi Coffee Workshop (Collingwood)

Cibi Coffee Workshop on Wellington Street has become Collingwood's morning meeting room. The single-origin matcha lineup is the headline, but the koji-cured salmon bagel is doing the work nobody talks about.

Groove (Abbotsford)

It shouldn't still be on a "where to line up" list six months in, but here we are. Groove on Lithgow Street is producing the salted cream coffee at a higher rate than it did during the FYP run, and the new matcha basque is the closest thing to a Melbourne institution-in-the-making this season.

Melbourne's Best New Cafes to Try in May 2026

Time Out Melbourne · 21 May 2026

May has brought a heavier-than-usual class of openings, plus one second-act return from North Melbourne. Here's where we've been queueing.

Marrowbone, Fitzroy

The Etta team's six-week seafood residency on Smith Street is exactly the kind of project that makes a Melbourne food month. Marrowbone's daily-changing menu is the headline; the bar is the move if you didn't book.

Kuro, Fitzroy North

Kuro on St Georges Road is the first Melbourne project from the Sydney team behind Kuro Bar. The natural-wine list is the draw, but the bar snacks — particularly the smoked-eel toast with horseradish — are what's keeping the regulars from leaving.

Assembly, North Melbourne

It has been five months since Assembly opened behind that North Melbourne roller door and the team has settled in. The Dunkaccino has become a calling card and the new Tina non-alcoholic wine flight is the next thing the city is going to start talking about.

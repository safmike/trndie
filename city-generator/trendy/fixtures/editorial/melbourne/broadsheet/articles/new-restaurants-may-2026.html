11 New Melbourne Bars, Bakeries and Restaurants Including a Seafood Pop-up in Fitzroy and a Matcha Haven in Collingwood

By Sofia Levin · 18 May 2026 · Broadsheet Melbourne

A run of new openings has landed across the inner north this month, and a handful are already worth rearranging a weekend for. Here's what the team has been eating, queueing for, and recommending to friends.

Marrowbone, Fitzroy

The team behind Etta have launched a six-week seafood residency on Smith Street under the name Marrowbone. The menu changes daily — yesterday it was Hervey Bay scallops with brown butter and finger lime; today it's a kingfish crudo with green almond and salted plum. Walk-ins only for the bar; the dining room is already booked out three weeks deep. Don't sleep on the soft-serve dessert — a salted-miso swirl with a brown-butter crumb that's quietly the best thing on the menu.

Cibi Coffee Workshop, Collingwood

The Japanese-Australian design studio Cibi has finally opened the matcha-focused cafe it has been teasing for a year. Cibi Coffee Workshop sits in a narrow Wellington Street shopfront with concrete benches, a single-origin matcha bar, and a brunch menu that runs the obligatory matcha tiramisu but also a genuinely strange (in a good way) hojicha onigiri.

Mörk Chocolate Brew House, North Melbourne

The drinking-chocolate specialists have reopened in a larger North Melbourne site after a year away. Mörk Chocolate Brew House still does the dense single-origin hot chocolates that built the original following, plus a new dessert program leaning into chocolate-and-savoury pairings (the smoked chocolate with anchovy butter on toast is the conversation piece).

Also worth a look this month: Kafka in Carlton North (an Italian bottle shop and wine bar that's been quietly excellent), and the second outpost of South Yarra's Hoshi in Windsor.

Where to Find Soft Serve in Melbourne, May 2026

By Jordan Kretchmer · 22 May 2026 · Broadsheet Melbourne

Melbourne's quiet obsession with the soft-serve machine continues. Here's where the city is queueing this month.

Marrowbone, Fitzroy

The Smith Street seafood pop-up keeps a single soft-serve dessert on the menu and rotates the flavour weekly. The current rotation — salted miso with a brown-butter crumb — is the strongest thing on a strong menu, and worth walking in just to order at the bar.

Auntie's Diner, Brunswick

Auntie's Diner opened on Sydney Road in March and quietly added a soft-serve machine in April. The pandan-coconut twist is the one to order; the team is also doing a weekly black-sesame run on Fridays.

Honourable mentions: Pidapipo's autumn rotation (kefir-and-rhubarb is doing real numbers), and the queue out the front of Standing Room on Saturdays.
